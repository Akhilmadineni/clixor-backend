Clixor is for coordinating shared activities and communicating respectfully. These guidelines apply to profiles, names, groups, messages, photos, files, invitations, expense notes, payment links, and other content or conduct on the service.

## Protect people

Do not threaten, bully, stalk, harass, dox, extort, or impersonate anyone. Do not promote violence or attack people on the basis of protected characteristics. Do not share private contact details, location, financial information, or images without permission or another lawful basis. Disagreements over an expense are not permission to threaten or expose someone.

## Protect children and privacy

Child sexual abuse or exploitation, grooming, trafficking, and sexual content involving minors are prohibited. Do not solicit intimate images from minors. Do not post nonconsensual intimate photos or videos, including altered or AI-generated depictions, or threaten to distribute them. Sexually explicit content is not permitted on Clixor, even if it is not otherwise unlawful.

Clixor is not for children under 13. Teens must have the parent or guardian permission described in the Terms of Service. Do not pressure someone to disclose their age, identity documents, authentication codes, location, or financial details in a group.

## Keep records and links trustworthy

Do not fabricate payments to deceive others, create phishing links, misrepresent an identity, spam invitations, or run scams. Verify payment destinations and confirm a payment with the recipient or provider. Never request someone else's bank password or login code through Clixor.

## Respect the service and others' rights

Do not upload malware, circumvent access controls, infringe intellectual property, scrape private user information, or disrupt the service. Share only content you have a right to share. Good-faith criticism, consumer reviews, lawful reporting, and independent ideas are not prohibited merely because we disagree with them.
