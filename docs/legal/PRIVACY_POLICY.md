## 3. Who receives information

- **Other participants:** authorized group members can see shared records and content. Supported discovery can expose limited profile information to people searching for you. Be careful about whom you invite and what you share.
- **Infrastructure providers:** Oracle Cloud hosts backend infrastructure and storage, and Cloudflare handles network delivery and protection. These functions involve processing data such as network identifiers, requests, stored records, and media as needed for the service. Infrastructure encryption is not the same as end-to-end encryption.
- **Apple and optional integrations:** Apple processes information for Apple sign-in, device services, and enabled notifications. Place-search features use Apple MapKit and, when configured, Google Places/Maps services; those providers receive queries and relevant place or location information. Optional SMS providers process phone numbers and delivery information only when an SMS feature is enabled and used.
- **Payment providers you open:** a payment link or app may receive recipient details, amount, or a payment note. The provider independently handles the payment and its own privacy obligations. Clixor's settlement record is not a payment-provider receipt.
- **Support, safety, and legal recipients:** authorized personnel and necessary service providers may process reports or relevant records to support users, protect the service, respond to valid legal process, or meet reporting and preservation duties. We may share information with advisers or authorities when reasonably necessary and legally permitted.
- **A business successor:** if the service is involved in a merger, sale, or similar transaction, information may be transferred subject to applicable law, appropriate protections, and required notice or consent. This clause does not authorize materially different data uses without the required process.

Independent services have their own notices. A provider's notice does not replace our responsibility for information we disclose to it.

## 5. Security and encryption boundaries

We use safeguards such as authenticated access and protected network communications. Supported chat encryption is intended to keep encrypted message payloads readable by authorized participant devices rather than the server. It does not make the entire app end-to-end encrypted: account information, group membership, delivery metadata, shared expense records, and some media are processed outside that protection. Content voluntarily included in a support or abuse report may also be readable by the recipient of the report.

No security measure guarantees absolute protection. Participants can copy or disclose information, and a compromised device can expose it. Access to encrypted history can depend on device keys and supported recovery capabilities; we cannot promise recovery of every lost message or key.

## 6. Retention and deletion

We retain account and shared-service information while it is needed for the features you use, subject to valid deletion requests and applicable law. Shared records may need to remain available to other participants. Security records, support requests, and legal evidence have different purposes and may require separate retention periods. Retention should be limited to what those purposes reasonably require, not kept indefinitely just because storage is available.

Use Profile → Delete Account to request deletion. The backend removes or anonymizes account identity, revokes sessions, and removes active memberships and discovery eligibility. Some shared records retain a deleted-user identifier to preserve group history. Personal media cleanup may occur through background processing rather than instantly. Shared records can still contain information that other users independently entered; contact us about specific material needing further review.

Deleting an account cannot recall recipient copies, screenshots, or independently saved files. Backups and legal-preservation records may not be erased immediately. Limited information may be retained where required by law or necessary for a specific lawful security or dispute purpose. A valid deletion request is not rejected merely because some limited retention is justified.
